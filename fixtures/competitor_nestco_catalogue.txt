PAGE 1 - ABOUT NESTCO
Founded in 2012 in Pune, NestCo runs three warehouses and stocks about 450 products across home decor, lighting and soft furnishings.

PAGE 2 - WARRANTY
All lamps and electrical items carry a 2-year warranty. Textiles carry a 6-month warranty. Claims are filed in the app and a replacement is shipped within 7 days.

PAGE 3 - RETURNS
NestCo offers 15-day returns. The customer pays return shipping unless the item is defective. Refunds are issued in 5-7 business days after the item is received.

Metro cities receive orders in 2 days; the rest of India in 5-7 days. Same-day delivery is available in Pune and Mumbai for orders placed before 11am. Shipping costs Rs 79 on orders under Rs 799.

Products use recycled cotton and FSC-certified wood. NestCo has shipped in plastic-free packaging since 2025.
