Name and logo options

Three directions for the product name. "Growth Agent" stays as the working name in the app until you choose; changing it later is a one-line edit.

My pick

Northline

Your growth, pointed the right way.

A trend line that ends in a compass needle: direction, not just data. Works as a favicon, calm and professional, and the arrow reads as "up and to the right" without being a cliché chart icon.

Good: Sounds like a serious analyst; pairs with the blue palette already used in the app.

Watch out: A little generic as a word; check for existing products with this name.

Upswell

Watch your numbers rise.

Three rising bars under a swell line, in a circle. Friendly and optimistic, the most obviously "growth".

Good: Easy to say and remember; the circle shape makes a good avatar and app icon.

Watch out: More consumer-feeling; less authority for a tool that delivers bad news honestly.

Lantern

Light on what is hiding in your numbers.

A lantern with an amber flame. It says "we show you what you could not see", which is the real job of the product: finding problems in your data and competitors.

Good: Most distinctive and memorable; the amber flame gives the brand a colour of its own.

Watch out: The least literal link to growth, so the tagline carries more weight.

Before you commit: I have not checked trademarks or domain names for any of these. Search for existing products and for a free domain before you settle. For a case study that is optional; for anything public it is essential. The logos are simple SVG drawings I made for this sheet, so they are placeholders to react to, not finished brand artwork.
